## The problem

You came back from the trip with 4,000 photos. Or the wedding. Or twelve years of phone backups.

Somewhere in there are the good ones. Finding them shouldn't take a whole weekend — or mean handing your files over to a giant photo library.

---

## How it works

### 1. Pick a folder
Any folder you already have. No import, no catalog, no "library". Your photos stay exactly where they are.

### 2. Tap a key
`0` Delete · `1` Unclassified · `2` Keep · `3` Star · `4` Select

One keystroke per photo. No clicks, no dialogs, no "are you sure?". Just flow.

### 3. See what's left
Filter by category with live counts. Show only your Stars, review your shortlist, done.

---

## Your files are sacred

NimblePhoto never moves, renames, copies or deletes a single photo. Not one.

Your choices are saved as good old **macOS Finder tags** — the colored dots you already know. So they show up in Finder, in Spotlight searches, and they travel with the file to any other Mac.

Uninstall NimblePhoto tomorrow and your tags are still there. That's the whole trick.

## Features

**⚡ Built for big piles**
Glides through 5,000 to 50,000+ photos without breaking a sweat.

**⌨️ Keyboard first**
Arrow keys to move, numbers to sort, Space to open. Your mouse can take the day off.

**🔍 Full-size viewer**
Open any photo, zoom to 100% to check the focus, then sort it without leaving the view.

**🎬 Videos too**
Clips play right alongside your photos. Same grid, same keys, one workflow.

**📷 Speaks every format**
JPEG, HEIC, PNG, WEBP, TIFF and RAW (CR2, DNG, RAF), plus MP4, MOV and M4V.

**🏷️ Your words, your categories**
Rename "Star" to "Portfolio" or "Select" to "Client picks". NimblePhoto updates the tags for you.

**🕰️ Always in order**
Sorted by the date the shot was taken, so every shoot tells its story from start to finish.

**ℹ️ Photo info at a glance**
Camera, lens, aperture, shutter speed, ISO, location — all the EXIF goodies in one tidy panel.

**📝 Copy all text**
Photographed a whiteboard, a menu or a receipt? Right-click and copy every word in it.

**👀 Stays in sync**
Add or remove files in Finder and NimblePhoto notices on its own.

## Private by design

No account. No cloud. No tracking. No subscription to your photos.

Everything happens on your Mac, inside Apple's App Store sandbox. NimblePhoto only sees the folder you show it.
